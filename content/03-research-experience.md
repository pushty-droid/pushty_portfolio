## 1. Research Intern (Master's Thesis) — Indian Institute of Astrophysics (IIA), Bengaluru

- **Dates:** 1 January 2026 – 31 July 2026 (content sheet). CV lists this as "2025–Present".
- **Programme:** Visiting Student Programme 2026, IIA
- **External supervisor:** Dr. Debika Chowdhury (DST INSPIRE Faculty Fellow, IIA)
- **Internal supervisor:** Dr. Gopal Kashyap (School of Advanced Sciences, VIT Vellore)

**What she did**

- Modelled the Stochastic Gravitational-Wave Background (SGWB) sourced by primordial magnetic fields in non-helical configurations.
- Derived analytic gauge-field solutions across three cosmological eras: pre-inflationary Bunch–Davies vacuum, inflation, and reheating.
- Computed the spectral energy density Ω_GW(f) and compared it against LISA, ET, DECIGO, BBO and aLIGO sensitivity curves.
- Carried out a rigorous backreaction consistency analysis, developing analytical and numerical solutions for magnetogenesis beyond the slow-roll regime.
- Identified the parameter space where sourced signals would be detectable by future observatories.

**Headline results:** Ω_GW ≈ 4.4 × 10⁻¹⁹ for n = 2; backreaction constraint Ω_EM ≈ 2.1 × 10⁻⁵ ≪ 1 satisfied. Full detail in `08-thesis-summary.md`.

## 2. Cosmic Distance Duality Relation (CDDR) and BAO Analysis — VIT Vellore

- **Dates:** 2025–2026
- **Supervisor:** Dr. Gopal Kashyap
- **Output:** Lead author, arXiv:2604.04436

**What she did**

- Engineered model-independent tests of the Cosmic Distance Duality Relation (CDDR) to probe directional anisotropies.
- Built a Python analysis pipeline (NumPy, Astropy, scikit-learn) reconstructing the duality ratio η(z) from Pantheon+ and SH0ES data.
- Applied Gaussian Process Regression (GPR) and MCMC sampling (`emcee`) for uncertainty propagation and dipole statistical inference.
- Cross-validated against DESI DR2 BAO angular diameter distances for joint expansion constraints and tests of departures from ΛCDM.
- Datasets used: Pantheon+, BAO from 6dFGS / SDSS / BOSS / eBOSS / DES / DESI DR1 & DR2, and cosmic chronometers.
- Demonstrated consistency with the Etherington reciprocity theorem within observational limits, while identifying potential signals of cosmic anisotropy in SN Ia positional distributions.

Full narrative in `05-featured-projects.md` (Project 1).
